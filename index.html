About Fitness Progression Paths

A training calculator for building endurance, strength and power toward an elite level without carrying mass you don't need. Everything you enter stays in this browser.

It is an educational planner, not medical advice. Get a professional assessment for pain, a recent injury, or a health condition before you train hard.
